# Business workflow

1. Customer opens the brochure QR destination at `/` and browses the catalog.
2. Customer selects a mix of products totaling at least five units, supplies name, phone, delivery address and map pin.
3. Server checks product availability/prices and straight-line distance from configured Titik Nol Dieng coordinates; greater than 8 km is rejected.
4. QRIS orders remain undispatched until verified settlement; COD is collected by the assigned internal courier upon handover.
5. Admin confirms/monitors the order, assigns courier, and oversees delivery. Customer tracks the order.

Current customer catalog and operational dashboards are incomplete. Do not represent steps 2–5 as available end-to-end yet.
